================================================================================
OPTIMAL TURN BUDGET: 6-8 TURNS FOR PASSING TASKS
================================================================================

PHASE 1: LOCATE & ORIENT (Turns 1-2)
- Turn 1: ls /app/resources/ [mandatory]
- Turn 2: grep for table name [optional, ~20% of cases]

PHASE 2: EXTRACT & COMPUTE (Turns 3-5)
- Turn 3: cat /app/resources/specific_page.txt [read table]
- Turn 4: python3 -c "extract + compute" [inline code]
- Turn 5: (optional) second python3 for complex math

PHASE 3: WRITE & VERIFY (Turns 6-8)
- Turn 6: echo -n "VALUE" > /app/answer.txt [MUST happen by turn 8]
- Turn 7: (optional) cat /app/answer.txt [verify written]

CRITICAL INSIGHT: Every turn after 8 reduces success rate by ~5%.

FAILURE ANALYSIS (v20: 75 fails out of 246)

Failure Mode Distribution:
- B: Prolonged search, wrong number    31 (41%)  → Extraction misread
- C: Quick fail, wrong number          20 (27%)  → Found wrong table

Average turns for failures: 18.2 (median 15)

IMPLICATION: Tasks thrashing past turn 10 are failure-destined. Force commit.

================================================================================
WHY v15 FAILED: SKILL LOADING OVERHEAD
================================================================================

v15 Workflow:
Turn 1:  load("officeqa-checklist")       [setup]
Turn 2:  load("officeqa-tools")           [setup]
Turn 3:  ls /app/resources/               [orientation]
Turn 4:  python3 g.py                     [fails: file not found]
Turn 5:  load("officeqa-table-parser")    [fetch script code]
Turn 6:  write g.py to /tmp/              [write script]
Turn 7:  python3 /tmp/g.py                [run script]
Turn 8-12: grep/sed/python to extract     [extract data]
Turn 13-15: compute + write answer        [compute + write]

Total: 15 turns (7 wasted on setup + recovery)

v20 Workflow (same task):
Turn 1:  ls /app/resources/               [orientation]
Turn 2:  (skip, file obvious)
Turn 3:  cat /app/resources/file.txt      [read]
Turn 4:  python3 -c "extract"             [extract]
Turn 5:  echo > /app/answer.txt           [write]

Total: 5 turns (all productive)

VERDICT: Skills kill performance. Inline forever.

================================================================================
TOP 3 OPTIMIZATIONS (Priority Order)
================================================================================

1. REDUCE PROMPT TO 3 LINES
   Current: "Write immediately: printf '%s' "VALUE" > /app/answer.txt"
   Add: "By turn 5, answer must be written. Can refine if needed."
   Effect: Forces commitment before token limit
   ROI: +2-4% (prevents 47 no-answer timeouts from v15)

2. INLINE CPI TABLE (400 bytes)
   Pre-embed 1913-2024 CPI-U values in prompt
   Eliminates 2-3 turns of external lookup
   ROI: +1-2%

3. ADD VERIFICATION STEP (Turn 5)
   After extraction, verify:
   - Row header matches query
   - Column header matches (outlays not receipts)
   - Units in expected range
   - Value consistent with neighbors
   ROI: +3-5% (fixes 50% of wrong-number fails)

Combined estimated gain: +8-15% (77-84% accuracy)

================================================================================
DON'T DO THIS (Common Mistakes)
================================================================================

- DO NOT use skills/load() again
  Proven worse: 50% vs 69.5% accuracy, +7 turns overhead

- DO NOT use MCP unless arena adds tool_definitions support
  Unreliable in arena, inline python3 works everywhere

- DO NOT embed full tables in prompt
  Too much context bloat, model can grep them anyway

- DO NOT require turn cap enforcement at model level
  Prompt incentives work better (e.g., "write by turn 5")

- DO NOT change max_turns to <25
  Want safety margin, but focus on turning earlier

- DO NOT add complex verification without testing
  Start simple (units check), add incrementally
