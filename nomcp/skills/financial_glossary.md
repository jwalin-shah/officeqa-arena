# Treasury Bulletin Financial Glossary

Common terms found in U.S. Treasury Bulletins (1939-2025). Use these definitions
to correctly interpret table headers and row labels.

## Budget Categories

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Budget receipts | Total federal government income (taxes, duties, fees) |
| Budget expenditures / Outlays | Total federal government spending |
| Surplus | Receipts > Expenditures (positive) |
| Deficit | Expenditures > Receipts (negative, often shown in parentheses) |
| Net receipts | Gross receipts minus refunds |

## Revenue Sources

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Internal revenue | All domestic taxes collected by IRS |
| Individual income taxes | Personal income tax |
| Corporation income taxes | Corporate income tax |
| Employment taxes | Social Security + Medicare (FICA) |
| Excise taxes | Taxes on specific goods (alcohol, tobacco, gasoline) |
| Estate and gift taxes | Inheritance and gift taxes |
| Customs duties | Tariffs on imported goods |

## Expenditure Categories

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| National defense | Military spending (DoD + atomic energy + related) |
| Veterans' Administration / Veterans Affairs | Benefits and services for veterans |
| Interest on the public debt | Interest payments on federal borrowing |
| Public works | Infrastructure (roads, dams, buildings) |
| Agricultural | Farm subsidies, crop insurance, soil conservation |

## Debt Instruments

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Treasury bills (T-bills) | Short-term (< 1 year) government securities |
| Treasury notes | Medium-term (2-10 year) government securities |
| Treasury bonds | Long-term (> 10 year) government securities |
| Savings bonds | Non-marketable securities sold to public |
| Public debt | Total outstanding federal government debt |
| Gross federal debt | Public debt held by public + intragovernmental |

## Trust Funds

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Highway Trust Fund | Funded by gas tax for roads/bridges |
| Social Security (OASI) | Old-Age and Survivors Insurance |
| Railroad Retirement | Pension system for railroad workers |
| Unemployment Trust Fund | State unemployment insurance reserves |

## Financial Metrics

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Currency in circulation | Physical money (coins + Federal Reserve notes) outside banks |
| Gold certificates | Certificates backed by gold held at Fort Knox/Fed |
| Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) | IMF reserve asset, international unit of account |
| Exchange Stabilization Fund (ESF) | Treasury fund for FX market intervention |

## Archaic Terms (1939-1960s)

| Term | Modern Equivalent |
|------|-------------------|
| War Department | Department of Defense (renamed 1947) |
| Navy Department | Merged into DoD in 1947 |
| Public debt service | Interest on the public debt |
| Alien property | Seized assets from foreign nationals |
| RFC (Reconstruction Finance Corporation) | Depression-era lending agency (dissolved 1957) |
| CCC (Commodity Credit Corporation) | Farm price support program |
| TVA (Tennessee Valley Authority) | Regional power/development authority |
| PWA (Public Works Administration) | New Deal infrastructure program |
| WPA (Works Progress Administration) | New Deal employment program |

## Units and Scaling

Tables declare units in their header line. ALWAYS check this before using values.

| Table header says | Raw value "1,234" means |
|-------------------|------------------------|
| (In thousands of dollars) | $1,234,000 |
| (In millions of dollars) | $1,234,000,000 |
| (In billions of dollars) | $1,234,000,000,000 |
| (In dollars) or no unit | $1,234 |

If a question asks "in millions" and the table is "in millions", use values as-is.
If a question asks "in nominal dollars" and the table is "in thousands", multiply by 1,000.

## Footnote Markers

Strip these from numeric values before computing:
- Superscript numbers: "1,580 3/" means 1580 (footnote 3)
- Asterisks: "1,580*" means 1580 (see footnote)
- "r/" or "R/" = revised figure
- "p/" or "P/" = preliminary figure
- "e/" = estimated
- Parentheses around a number = negative value: "(500)" = -500
