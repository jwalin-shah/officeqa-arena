description: Fiscal year boundaries and budget proposal release dates
---

FY pre-1977: Jul 1 (Y-1) to Jun 30 (Y). FY1953 = Jul 1952–Jun 1953.
FY post-1976: Oct 1 (Y-1) to Sep 30 (Y). FY2024 = Oct 2023–Sep 2024.
Transition quarter: Jul-Sep 1976.
CY: Jan 1 to Dec 31.

Budget proposals typically released in February of the prior CY.
FY bulletins: pre-1977 look at Sep bulletin, post-1976 look at Dec bulletin.
Bulletin year ≠ data year. A 1941 bulletin reports FY1940 data.
